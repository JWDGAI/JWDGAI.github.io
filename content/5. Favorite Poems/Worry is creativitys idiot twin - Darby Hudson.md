Worry is creativitys idiot twin
They both make shit up from nothing.
One gives you a panic attack, the other
A painting. Same engine, different
Destinations. Everyones got the ability to
Turn nothing into something. But most use
Their magical brain to waste it on petty
nonsense, because the world sells endless
fear to mistake as excitement. Early on I
realised I couldnt loan my spark to that
circus. Instead, I used it for art.
And I got bolder -- Against all reason I
stopped worrying about the bigger stuff --
Bills, jobs, dying alone. I just quit.
And thats when I had all this extra room in
my head for actual magic. And finally the
bills paid themselves, the jobs worked
themselves, life took care of itself.
It always has. And now I was alone with
new music, perfect stupid and a blazing
army of stars.
