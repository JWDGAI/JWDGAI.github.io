
## Each person has a dragon - J. Steffen

Each person has a dragon in their mind
Which weaves and twines within the subtle brain
In shy seclusion, hiding from the pain
Of memories that ache and throb and wind
Through ducts and shafts unforgetfulness,
Through pools and streams of vivid yesterdays,
In search of sanctuary from the rays
Of its deep knowledge of its loneliness

Each person has a dragon. Some are cold,
And lurk in freezing caverns all alone,
Their ragged mouths shut tight on truths untold,
Eternally consumed by their own heat,
Their hearts exploding to the searing beat
Of always unfulfillable desire.

Each person has a dragon, flame or ice,
Or even both at once -- each secret soul
Protects the agony that makes it whole,
The serpent at the heart of paradise.
Each person has a dragon: none can fly.
Nor can they sleep. Or die. Or even lie.
